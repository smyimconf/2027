About

A meeting for the people building the field

Since 2014, SMYIM has gathered early-career soft matter scientists and engineers from across India to share their research, argue about where the field is heading, and start new collaborations.

The meet brings together physicists, chemists, biologists and engineers working on colloids, polymers and gels, granular and active matter, liquid crystals, interfaces, rheology and non-equilibrium phenomena. Talks are by invitation, and mentoring sessions with senior scientists cover research directions, grant writing and career development.

Alongside faculty members and industry scientists, a limited number of places are kept for postdoctoral researchers working in soft matter. A few talk slots are still open; if you’d like to be considered, write to the organisers.
