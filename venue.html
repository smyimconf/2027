Venue

Sinclairs Retreat, Kalimpong

A boutique resort on five acres of landscaped grounds above Kalimpong, looking straight out at the Himalayan range and a forested valley.

Kalimpong sits at about 1,250 m on a ridge above the Teesta river, an old hill station on the trade route between Bengal and Tibet. In late May the days are mild, typically in the high teens to mid-twenties Celsius.

By air
Bagdogra Airport (IXB), near Siliguri, then about 3 hours by road.
By rail
New Jalpaiguri (NJP), then about 3 hours by road along the Teesta.
Address
6th Mile, Purbong Basti, Kalimpong 734301, West Bengal
Open in Google Maps   Hotel website

Close by: Deolo Hill and Dr Graham’s Homes, the 1692 Thongsa Gompa, Durpin Monastery, and the orchid nurseries the town is known for.
